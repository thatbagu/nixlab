# Machines

The hardware your lab runs on: adding and replacing machines, and what they can do besides running apps.

- **[Adding Nodes](./adding-nodes.md):** install, add, replace, reinstall or remove a machine over SSH.
- **[Migrating to New Hardware](./migrate.md):** move the whole lab onto new machines.
- **[Encryption at Rest](./encryption.md):** encrypted disks, unlocked by the TPM, with remote unlock.
- **[NAS](./nas.md):** a ZFS pool with SMB and NFS shares on a machine's extra disks.
- **[Power](./power.md):** a clean shutdown when the power fails, and Wake-on-LAN.

Install trouble? See [Installing on Difficult Hardware](./hardware-quirks.md).
