# Apps

Everything about the apps in your lab: ready-made ones, your own, and the passwords they need.

- **[App Catalog](./catalog.md):** popular self-hosted apps, each turned on with one line in `vars.nix`.
- **[Adding Apps](./adding-charts.md):** any container image, Compose file or Helm chart, on Kubernetes or directly on a machine.
- **[Apps That Change Their Own Config](./self-mutating-apps.md):** Home Assistant and friends: what goes in your repo, and what gets backed up.
- **[Passwords and Keys](./secrets-basics.md):** your key, seeing and changing passwords, and your apps' secrets.

Going further: [Apps in Depth](./app-modules.md) has every option, and [Customizing nixlab](./customizing.md) shows how to change nixlab's own apps.
